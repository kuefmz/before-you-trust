# Brand Guide — Before You Trust

## Brand idea
**Before You Trust** helps people replace blind trust with informed judgment.

The brand should feel protective without being paranoid, serious without being frightening, and investigative without feeling like surveillance.

## Primary tagline
**Know what the internet already knows.**

## Supporting lines
- Search the web like you wish you had before.
- Information first. Decisions second.
- Trust shouldn't start with a blind spot.
- Public information. Clear sources. Your decision.

## Positioning statement
For people considering whether to trust someone, Before You Trust is an evidence-first public-web research tool that gathers relevant public information, verifies claims, separates identities, and shows original sources so users can make better-informed decisions.

Unlike opaque people-search sites, it does not hide weak results behind alarming paywalls, invent risk scores, or pretend that lack of information proves safety.

## Personality
- Calm
- Evidence-first
- Respectful
- Clear
- Protective
- Non-judgmental
- Transparent

## Voice
### Do
- Say what was found and where it came from.
- Explain uncertainty clearly.
- Use neutral language.
- Encourage users to inspect the original source.
- Say "No reliable information found" when appropriate.

### Don't
- Call someone dangerous, trustworthy, a scammer, abusive, or criminal unless reporting an authoritative adjudicated fact and clearly attributing it.
- Use clickbait such as "shocking records found".
- Treat allegations as facts.
- Imply a complete background check.
- Promise safety.

## Visual direction
A dark navy foundation communicates seriousness and trust. Bright electric blue signals search, clarity, and technology. White/off-white keeps the product accessible and readable. Green is reserved for verified/corroborated information; red is reserved for conflicts or items requiring attention, never for a generalized person-level "risk score".

### Palette
- Midnight Navy — `#081320` — primary dark background
- Evidence Blue — `#2563EB` — primary action
- Clear Sky — `#60A5FA` — secondary accent
- Verified Green — `#10B981` — verified/corroborated status
- Attention Red — `#EF4444` — conflicts/important caveats
- Cloud — `#F1F5F9` — soft background
- Ink — `#111827` — primary text
- White — `#FFFFFF`

## Typography
Recommended: **Inter**.
- Headings: Inter 700/800
- Body: Inter 400/500
- Labels/status: Inter 600

## Logo concept
A shield + search lens + human silhouette. The shield means protection; the magnifier means research; the person silhouette keeps the focus on identity verification.

Avoid police-badge aesthetics, fingerprint clichés, red warning triangles as the main logo, or imagery suggesting stalking/surveillance.

## UI status language
Preferred labels:
- Verified
- Corroborated
- Likely match
- Possible match
- Unverified
- Conflicting information
- Public allegation
- Official record
- No reliable information found
